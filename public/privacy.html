Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This site (devbyamr.com) is a personal portfolio belonging to Amr HA. This policy explains what little data the site touches and how it's used.

What this site does not do

  • It does not use cookies.
  • It does not run analytics or advertising trackers.
  • It does not have a contact form or account system, so it never collects your name, email, or any personal details through the site itself.

Local storage

The site remembers your chosen language (English/Arabic) using your browser's local storage. This stays on your device only, is never sent to any server, and you can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.
